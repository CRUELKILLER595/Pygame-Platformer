PixelBound — Platform Enemies Pack
====================================

INSTALL
-------
Extract this ZIP into the ROOT of your PixelBound project. It creates:
    assets/Enemies/*.png

Do not rename the PNGs yet; the loader will use a filename-to-frame map.
